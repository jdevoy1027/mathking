Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

MathKing does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from anyone — including children. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers of any kind.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the MathKing website at mathking.pro and to the MathKing app for iOS and iPadOS. Both are referred to below as “MathKing”.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, and have no way to receive, a name, email address, age, location, photograph, contact list, device identifier, or any other personal information.

Specifically, MathKing contains:

  • No sign-up, sign-in, or user accounts
  • No analytics or usage measurement of any kind, first-party or third-party
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No cookies used for tracking
  • No social media integrations or share buttons
  • No links that leave the app
  • No in-app purchases and no payment processing
  • No microphone, camera, photo library, contacts, or location access

Information stored on your device

MathKing saves two small settings so the app behaves sensibly between sessions: whether sound is switched on, and your best score. On the website these are held in the browser’s local storage; in the app they are held in the app’s own private settings.

This information never leaves the device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, it is not linked to any identity, and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, removes it permanently.

Children’s privacy

MathKing is designed for children practising early arithmetic, and we have built it so that the question of children’s data does not arise: no personal information is collected from any user, of any age.

Because we collect no personal information from children, there is nothing for a parent to review, delete, or consent to under the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or comparable laws. In keeping with Apple’s requirements for apps aimed at children, MathKing includes no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no links out of the app.

Network use

The iOS app makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. The website downloads the page itself when you visit it, and nothing further; there are no calls to third-party servers, fonts, scripts, or content delivery networks.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — may record standard server request logs such as IP address and browser type. That logging is performed by GitHub, not by us; we do not have access to it, and we do not analyse it. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information, because we do not have any to sell, rent, trade, or share.

Changes to this policy

If MathKing ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. If a future version were ever to collect personal information, we would say so clearly here before that version was released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@mathking.pro.
